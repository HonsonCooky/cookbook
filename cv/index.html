About

Software engineering seems a strange place to end up, coming from what some would jest is a singing cult. Years on stage alongside friends and family left a mark I have never shaken: a desire to contribute to productions only made possible because of every person's contributions. A knack for calculus and a quiet fascination with technology sent me toward completing a Bachelor of Engineering at Victoria University. My career since has spanned a wide variety of environments, showing me how software delivers value, and the multitude of difficulties that come with it. Like a musician, I practice in my free time. Exploring, experimenting, and casually reinforcing the elements that enable me to make small improvements every day.
