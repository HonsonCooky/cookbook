About

Software engineering seems a strange place to end up, coming from what friends would jest is a singing cult. Years on stage in ambitious productions showed me how amazing things happen when everyone energetically contributes their small part. A knack for calculus pulled me toward a Bachelor of Engineering at Victoria University. From there came my 5+ years of building in the industry, learning how software delivers value and everything that gets in its way. My craft doesn't stop when work does; like a musician, I keep practising in my free time, lately by building my own game engines in C on Raylib. Every day is a chance to be better than yesterday, a challenge I relish.
