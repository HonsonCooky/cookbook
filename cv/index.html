Software engineering seems a strange place to end up, coming from what friends would jest is a singing cult. I spent years on stage alongside friends and family, ultimately pulled away by a knack for calculus and a strange draw toward technology. Yet, I've found the toughest battles in this industry are much like a stage production. A definitive output always has to be delivered, but some shared vision of the future is the only thing that aligns everyone to pull something extraordinary together. Because I'm naturally drawn to this craft, it tends to spill over into my free time as well. Like a musician, I practice - exploring, experimenting, and reinforcing. It is all just quiet practice, refining my own approach to the craft.
